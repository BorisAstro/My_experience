# Teaching

I have taught photogrammetry, LiDAR, cartography, geodesy and geospatial programming since 2016, at undergraduate and graduate level in Bolivia, and as an invited instructor in Peru and Mexico. At the Escuela Militar de Ingeniería I hold faculty rank Category D.
