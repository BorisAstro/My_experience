# Research

My applied research sits where geodesy, photogrammetry and LiDAR meet production cartography: how map projections distort the surfaces we bill and regulate, how airborne and mobile sensors can be pushed to centimeter accuracy, and how those products feed 3D city models and autonomous-vehicle maps.

## Research Areas

- HD mapping for autonomous vehicles (Lanelet2 / OSM lane networks)
- Airborne and mobile LiDAR: classification, strip adjustment, DTM/DSM
- Photogrammetry: aerial triangulation, stereo-satellite DEM, SfM and UAV/RPAS
- Geodesy, map projections and local topographic planes
- GIS, remote sensing and artificial intelligence applied to point clouds and imagery
- 3D geospatial modeling, virtual reality and 3D printing from geospatial data
